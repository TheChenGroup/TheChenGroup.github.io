# Research Highlights

<ResearchCard image="research/2024-spin-penalty.avif">
  ### Spin-symmetry-enforced deep learning quantum Monte Carlo

  Optimiztions in deep learning quantum Monte Carlo methods are not guaranteed to produce quantum states with the desired spin symmetry. This work introduces a low-scaling penalty term that enforces spin symmetry with minimal computational overhead, achieving higher accuracy and efficiency for excited-state calculations.

  - [Z. Li *et al.* Nat Comput Sci 4, 910 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43588-024-00730-4)
</ResearchCard>
